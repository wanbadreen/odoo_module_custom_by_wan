MotoGene Promotion Engine

Configure reusable sales promotions with validity dates, eligible products, purchase conditions and rewards.

  • Free product rewards based on quantity or purchase amount.
  • Lucky Draw entries calculated from eligible purchases.
  • Scratch & Win card entitlement and serial allocation on confirmed orders.
  • Card serial history on Sales Orders and Delivery Orders.
  • Physical card returns recorded through the standard Return and Validate workflow.

Cards received back are marked Void — Returned when the return is completed. Voided serials remain in the history and cannot be allocated again.

Refer to the repository README for configuration details and test scenarios.
